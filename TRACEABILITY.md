# Requirements Traceability Matrix

This matrix maps the normalized requirements in `ENGINEERING_PLAN.md` to the work in `TASKS.md`. Planned and completed evidence are identified explicitly; approving requirement scope does not by itself verify implementation.

All requirement statuses were initialized as **NOT STARTED**.

## Status definitions

- **NOT STARTED:** Required work and evidence have not been implemented or accepted.
- **IN PROGRESS:** An approved task is being executed.
- **IN REVIEW:** Implementation and evidence await engineer review.
- **VERIFIED:** Required tests and validation evidence pass, but final acceptance is pending.
- **ACCEPTED:** The engineer has reviewed and accepted the requirement and its evidence.
- **BLOCKED:** Work cannot proceed until an identified decision, dependency, or external condition is resolved.
- **NOT APPLICABLE:** The engineer explicitly excluded a conditional requirement from scope and recorded the rationale.

## Maintenance Rules

1. Update a row when its mapped requirement, task, component, test, documentation, or evidence changes.
2. Do not advance status based only on AI assertion; link reproducible evidence and engineer review.
3. Conditional requirements may become **NOT APPLICABLE** only through an explicit engineer decision with rationale.
4. A requirement becomes **VERIFIED** only after its required tests and validation pass.
5. A requirement becomes **ACCEPTED** only after the engineer reviews the evidence and approves it.
6. Rejected or superseded AI output must remain traceable rather than being presented as accepted work.
